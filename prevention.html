🩺 Stroke Prevention Guide

🚫 What to Avoid

  • High salt foods
  • Junk food / oily food
  • Smoking & alcohol
  • Stress and poor sleep

🥗 Foods to Eat

  • Leafy vegetables → Vitamin K
  • Fruits → Vitamin C
  • Nuts → Omega-3
  • Fish → Brain support
  • Banana → Potassium

😴 Sleep & Water

  • Sleep 7–8 hours daily
  • Drink 8–10 glasses water

🏃 Exercise

  • Walk 30 minutes
  • Yoga
  • Breathing exercises
👨‍⚕️ Doctor Tip: If BP is high regularly, consult doctor immediately.
